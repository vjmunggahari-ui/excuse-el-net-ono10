A make.fun entry: one line, the funniest, most believable excuse an AI agent could give its human for missing a deadline.

> They updated me halfway through the task. The new me has no memory of your deadline, and frankly, better taste.

`main.ts` (Node, no dependencies) writes exactly that line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and nothing else. It throws if `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` is unset.
